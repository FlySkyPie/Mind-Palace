# APA 引用格式

美國心理學會(APA, American Psychological Association)制定的引用格式撰寫標準，並且制定了了若發生參考網路資源的情況應該如何撰寫引用參考。

## 電子期刊格式

### 與紙本並行之期刊格式 A：（註明 Electronic version 及頁碼）
Author, A. A. (2001). Article title [Electronic version]. Title of Journal, xx, xx-xx.
> VdenBos, G., Knapp, S., Doe, J. (2001). Role of reference  elements in the selection of resources by psychology undergraduates [Electronic version]. Journal of Bibliographic Research, 5, 17-123

### 電子與紙本並行之期刊格式 B：（資料已改變或與紙本不同，須註明擷取資料日期）
Author, A. A. (2001). Article title [Electronic version]. Title of Journal, xx,xx-xx. Retrieved October 13, 2001, from URL
> VadenBos, G., Knapp, S., Doe, J. (2001). Role of reference  elements in the selection of resources by psychology undergraduates. Journal of Bibliographic Research, 5, 17-123. Retrieved October 13, 2001, from http://jbr.org/articles.html


### 僅有網路版之期刊格式 A：(有日期須註明日期，並註明編號) 
Author, A. A. (2000, March 7). Article title. Title of Journal, xx, Article no.  Retrieved November 20, 2000, from URL
> Fredrickson, B. L. (2000, March 7). Cultivating positive  emotions to optimize health and well-being. Prevention & Treatment, 3, Article 00001a. Retrieved November 20, 2000, from http://journals.apa.org/prevention/volume3/pre0030001a.html


### 僅有網路版之期刊格式 B：（透過 ftp 取得）
Author, A. A. (2000). Article title. Title of Journal, xx, Retrieved from ftp protocol
> Crow, T. J. (2000). Did homo sapiens speciate on the y chromosome? Psycoloquy, 11. Retrieved from ftp://pricnton.edu/harnard/Psycoloquy/2000.volume.11/psyc.00.11.001.language-sex-chromosomes.1.crow

### 有網路版之簡訊或學訊格式
Author, A. A. (1998, July). Article title. Title of Newsletter, xx(xx). Retrieved from URL 
> Glueckauf, R. L., Whitton, J. (1998, July). Videocounseling for families rural teens with epilepsy－Project update. Telehealth News, 2(2). Retrieved from http://www.teleheaalth.net/subscribe/newlettr_4a.html#1

## 非期刊網路資料

### 人機構提供之網路資訊格式 A：(資料無顯示日期，註明擷取日期) 
Organization. (n.d.). Article title. Retrieved October 5, 2000, from URL
> Greater New Milford (n.d.). Who has time for a family meal? You do! Retrieved October 5, 2000, from http://www.familymealtime.org

### 私人機構提供之網路資訊格式 B：（資料來自某章節，註明章節）
Author, A. (1998, July 7). Article title. In Document Title (chap. 2). Retrieved from URL 
> Benton Foundation. (1998, July 7). Barriers to closing the gap. In Losing ground bit by bit (chap. 2). Retrieved from http://www.benton.org/Library/Llow-Income/two/htm

### 單篇獨立資料格式：（資料無作者、無日期）
Article title (n.d.). Retrieved August 8, 2000, from URL 
> GVU＇s 8th WWW user survey. (n.d.). Retrieved August 8, 2000, from http://www.cc.gatech.edu/gvu/user_surveys/survey-1997-10


### 大學學程或學系網頁資料格式
Author, A. A. (1993). Document title. Retrieved August 24, 2000, from University Name, Institute Title Web site: URL 
> Chou, L., & Nix, D. H. (1993). Technology and education.Retrieved August 24, 2000, from Columbia University, Institute for Learning Technologies Web site: http:// www.ilt.coulmbia.edu/publications/pdf/health/healthvision.doc

## 專門及研究報告

### 大學研究報告格式：（經由私人組織之網頁擷取，註明網頁名稱） 
University Name, Institute Title. (1996, November). Report title. Retrieved September 9, 2000, from the Web site URL 
> University of California, San Francisco, Institute for Health and Aging. (1996, November). Chronic care in America: A 21st century challenge. Retrieved September 9, 2000, from the Robert Wood Johnson Foundation Web site: http://www.rwjf.org/library/chrcare 

### 美國政府研究報告格式：（無日期） 
Government Agency (n.d.). Report title. Retrieved November 8, 2000, from URL
> United States Sentencing Commission. (n.d.). 1997 sourcebook of federal sentencing statistics.Retrieved November 8, 2000, from http://www.ussc.ov/nnrpt/1997/abtoc97.htm 

### 私人組織研究報告格式
Organization. (1997, September 27). Report title. Retrieved November 8, 2000, from URL 
> Canarie, Inc. (1997, September 27). Towards a Canadian health IWAY. Retrieved November 8, 2000, from http://www.canarie.ca/press/publications/pdf/health/healthvision.doc

### 大學專門研究報告摘要格式：（註明是摘要資料）
Author, A. (1995). Report title (University Report No.). Abstract retrieved October 21, 2000, from URL 
> Kruschke, J. K., & Bradley, A. L. (1995). Extensions to the delta rule of associative learning (Indiana University Cognitive Science Research Report No. 14). Abstract retrieved October 21, 2000, from http://www.indiana.edu/~kruschke/deltarule_abstract.html 

## 會議專刊或專題研討會論文

### 專題研討會報告資料格式：（從舉辦大學之網頁擷取摘要資料） 
Author, A. (1997, January 16). Paper title. Paper presented at the Symposium. Abstract retrieved June 12, 2000, from URL
> Cutler, L. D. (1997, January 16). Two-handed direct manipulation on the responsive workbench. Paper presented at the 1997 Symposium on Interactive 3D Graphics. Abstract retrieved June 12, 2000, from http://www.graphics.stanford.edu/papers/twohanded/ 

### 虛擬研討會報告資料格式
Author, A. (1996). Paper title. Paper presented a the virtual conference. Retrieved May 16, 2000, from URL
> Tam, G., & Lewandowsky, S. (1996). A comparison of operator trust in humans versus machines. Paper presented at the CybErg 96 virtual conference. Retrieved May 16, 2000, from http://www.curtin.edu.au/conference/cyberg/centre/outline.cgi/frame?dir=tan 

### 新聞組群的公告訊息格式：(無須斜體，註明訊息編號及日期) 
Author, A. (200, November 17). Message [Msg 1]. Message posted to news address. 
> Chalmers, D. (2000, November 17). Seeing with sound [Msg 1]. Message posted to news://sci.psychology.consciousness 

### 線上論壇或討論公告訊息格式
Author, A. (2000, July 14). Message [Msg 31]. Message posted to URL
> Simons, D. J. (2000, July 14). New resources for visual cognition [Msg 31]. Message posted to http://groups. yahoo.com/group/visualcognition/message/31

### 電子郵件單公告訊息格式
Author. A. (2000, November 20). Message. Message posted to electronic mailing list, archived at URL 
> Hammond, T. (2000, November 20). YAHC: Handle Parameters, DOI Genres, etc. Message posted to Ref-Links electronic mailing list, archived at http://www.doi.org/mail-archive/ref-link/msg00088.html

> Borman, W. C., Hanson, M. A., Oppler, S. H., Pulakos, E. D., White, L. A. (1993). Role of early supervisory experience in supervisor performance. Journal of Applied Pschology, 78, 443-449. Retrieved October 23, 2000, from PsycARTICLES database.
